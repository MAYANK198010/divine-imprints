Workflow Process

Production Process Flowchart

Systematic workflow pathways ensuring strict quality controls from raw stock feed to finished packaging and bound books.

Offset Printing & Book Binding Flow

  • 1
    Printing (Press) - Offset plate exposure & multi-color printing.
  • 2
    Folding - 4 Folding machines (Heidelberg Stahl & Polygraph).
  • 3
    Gathering - Muller Martini 24-station gathering machine.
  • 4
    Perfect Binding / Sewing - Six clamp perfect binder or thread sewing.
  • 5
    3-Cutter Trimming - SDY-2 3-Knife Trimmer precision edge trim.
  • 6
    Packing - Automatic shrink packing → Finished Book.

Packaging & Box Manufacturing Flow

  • 1
    Printing (Press) - High-resolution carton and packaging press runs.
  • 2
    Cutter (Die Cutting) - Precision die cutting for custom box shapes.
  • 3
    Pasting (Glueing) - High-speed automatic box pasting machine.
  • 4
    Inspection & Shrink Wrapping - Batch checking → Finished Box.
